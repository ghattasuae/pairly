Please read this agreement carefully. You accept it by posting the sign-off comment requested by the CLA bot on your pull request. It applies to all your past and future Contributions to the Project.

1. **"You"** means the individual who signs this agreement.
2. **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including changes or additions to existing work, that You submit to the Project by any means, for example a pull request, a patch, an issue comment containing code, or documentation.
