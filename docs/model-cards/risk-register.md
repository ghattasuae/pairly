# AI risk register

The risks of Pairly's AI components, what reduces each one in code, and what is left. Likelihood and impact are judgements, written as low, medium or high, not measurements. Eval numbers are in the [model cards](README.md), generated from the committed result files. Review this register when a component, a prompt or a threshold changes.

| # | Risk | Components | Likelihood | Impact | Controls in code | What is left |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | An AI step decides an outcome on its own (a rejection, a payment, a bank change) | all | low | high | Graphs stop at an interrupt; `resume_run` needs a stored human decision; the executor re-checks approvals, payload hash, people and second factor before any final action (`pairly_core.approvals.executor`) | None known; covered by tests that forge resumes and statuses |
| R2 | Unfair scores across protected groups | rubric scoring, CV parsing | medium | high | Blind redaction before scoring; self-identification never reaches scoring (import contracts); counterfactual fairness runs; bias report with impact ratios; calibration | The live fairness run shows score changes on some pairs, also with identical input (model run-to-run variation); no threshold set yet |
| R3 | Scores that do not match what people would give | rubric scoring | medium | medium | Scores are suggestions with evidence; people decide; overrides are recorded and feed calibration; low confidence escalates or leaves the criterion unscored | Agreement is measured on synthetic labels only |
| R4 | Prompt injection in a document changes a score or an extracted value | rubric scoring, CV parsing, invoice extraction | medium | high | Deterministic detectors, sanitising before any model, the document classifier in Hire, data-block framing, flagged documents go to a person | Paraphrased instructions are caught only by the classifier, which did not catch every one in the live run on a small attack set (see the [document classifier card](document-injection-classifier.md)) |
| R5 | Wrong value read from an invoice (amount, VAT, bank details) | invoice extraction | medium | high | Evidence quotes checked in code; low confidence goes to the exceptions queue; arithmetic, VAT, duplicate and bank checks; bank details never change from an invoice; a person approves every invoice | Header and VAT accuracy from the live run are below line-item accuracy; many invoices still need a person (see the [invoice extraction card](invoice-extraction.md)) |
| R6 | Personal data leaks through a model output or a trace | drafting components, all traced calls | low | high | Drafting prompts get no personal data; `check_draft` and `check_note` refuse contact details and unknown names; PII redaction in logs and traces, counted per organisation | Redaction is pattern based and tuned to over-redact; unusual formats could pass |
| R7 | Hallucinated evidence (a quote that is not in the document) | CV parsing, rubric scoring, invoice extraction, policy check | medium | medium | `quote_exists` and evidence location; a value or finding without valid evidence gets confidence 0 or is dropped | None known for quotes; the meaning of a valid quote can still be misread |
| R8 | A generated text is inappropriate or biased (job ad, message, kit) | JD drafting, message personalisation, interview kits, debrief and reference summaries | medium | medium | Deterministic checks (inclusive language, protected characteristics, links, contact details); a person edits and approves before anything is sent or published | Checks are pattern based |
| R9 | Model outage or budget exhaustion blocks work | all | medium | low | Fail closed to a person; template fallbacks for drafts; per-organisation budget with alerts | Work slows down; nothing is decided wrongly |
| R10 | Over-reliance: people approve what the AI suggests without reading | rubric scoring, invoice extraction | medium | high | Evidence next to every suggestion; blind mode; calibration without seeing AI scores; override rates per workflow on the AI governance board | Depends on the organisation's practice |
| R11 | Policy check misses a relevant rule | policy check | medium | medium | No policy found is not a pass; dropped findings force review; deterministic rules run too | Only as complete as the uploaded policy documents; no eval yet |
| R12 | GL code suggested wrongly | GL coding suggestion | medium | low | Codes must exist in the lists in force; the model suggestion is never applied by itself; corrections feed back | No eval yet |
| R13 | Replay does not reproduce a decision | all | low | medium | Temperature 0, recorded responses kept, replay flags differences and non-deterministic models | A live replay of a model without temperature support is marked not deterministic |

Owner: the organisation's AI governance lead, using the AI governance board (`pairly_core.dashboards.governance`: eval gate, usage, overrides, versions in production, guardrail findings, redaction counts, fairness runs, calibration).
